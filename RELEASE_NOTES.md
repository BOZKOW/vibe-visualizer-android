Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.50.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.49.2 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **One design: Glass.** The Android design is gone; every screen and every page of Settings is Glass. If you used the Android design, the app moves you over by itself.
- **Real frosted glass on the floating bars.** As you scroll Settings, the search and each page's title turn into islands that show what's under them blurred, with a light edge, a soft highlight along the top and a shadow. Their close and back buttons sit on glass too, and so does the kind's icon on every notice.
- **Notices in Settings sit in their place.** They're now under the search (or under a page's title), as wide as the search, instead of lying over the title on the left; as you scroll they float with the search.
- **Motion sickness** (Settings → Performance & battery). Less movement on screen: the lyrics stay where they are and change line with a soft fade instead of scrolling, the words fill in without bouncing, lines don't fly in or out, long names end in "…", the background holds still, and screens change without sliding. The lyrics keep exact time.
- **Sliding text stays in its own place.** A long song name no longer slides over the small cover beside it (or past the cover's edge on its side), and a long notice no longer slides into its icon. The edges fade only while there's more text past them, so the first and last letters show whole.
- **Landscape, better balanced.** The cover is smaller (at most 40% of the width, centred), leaving more room for the lyrics; the visualizer is back inside the margin instead of running off the right edge; and the blurred lyrics no longer reach over the cover's edge.
- **Your own lyrics files read as written.** "la-la-la" stays "la-la-la" (no spaces were to be added inside a hyphenated word), and background vocals show their brackets only with Settings → Lyrics → Background vocals in brackets on; off, the "(…)" a file comes with is left out cleanly.
- **Tap anywhere on a setting.** A switch, a choice, or a row with one button (like Choose cover image… or the new Choose canvas video… row, which open your gallery) now reacts to a tap anywhere on its tile, not only on the switch or the button. Rows with nothing to show no longer leave an empty tile.
