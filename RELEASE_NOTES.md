Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.39.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.38.4 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Lyrics style

- **Settings → Lyrics → Lyrics style**: pick one of Spicy Lyrics (every word fills, grows, lifts and glows), Spicy Lyrics Simple (the words only fill) or Focus. It replaces the separate Simple lyrics mode and Focus mode switches; what you had set carries over.

### Focus

- **The line being sung is in the middle** (it sat near the top with the bars on).
- **Breaks show their dots**, and a line sung to the end stays until the next one comes in: the intro, short breaks and the gaps between lines were an empty screen.

### Two lines at once

- **Two lines sung at the same time are placed together** (a duet, or overlapping lines as some Spicy Lyrics syncs have): both in view, around the middle; when they're taller than the screen, the two rows being sung stay in view and follow the singing. Before, keeping the newer line in view pushed the other one off the top.

### On its side

- **Bigger**: without a Canvas, the cover is as tall as the screen again, with the song's name in its big size and the seek bar over its foot; with a Canvas, a bigger cover, the big name and a wide seek bar. The bars are a strip on the right, above the credits.

### Also

- **Quick import**: the buttons under the controls can be "Add a lyrics file for this song" or "Play songs from your phone", and Your lyrics files are right under the sources in Settings → Lyrics.
- **Good news goes by itself**: white notices ("Up to date", "In sync", "Copied"…) go after 5 seconds; red and yellow ones stay until OK.
- **A button set to Lyrics or Visualizer** (gone with their screens) is now the bars and Lyrics timing in Settings too, not only on the player.
- **Every setting was tried on the test phone**, each of its values, with nothing crashing.
