Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.47.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.46.4 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Smoother animations, song changes most of all.** The seek bar told Android its position for accessibility on every frame. With any accessibility service on (a lot of phones have one: a "Dynamic Island" app, a gesture or automation app, a password manager), Android then went over the whole screen again each time, which took a large share of the phone's time and made animations stutter. It now tells it in whole percents, about once every two seconds. A song change also sends the new cover to the graphics card as soon as it's loaded, instead of in the first frame that shows it, and checking whether two song names match no longer rebuilds its patterns every time.
- **Long text slides to its end and back.** Notices, song names, artists and the top line, when they're too long, slide until their end is readable, wait, and slide back to the start, instead of running off the side and coming round again.
- **The visualizer takes its colours from the Canvas the whole time it plays,** not only from its first few seconds: it looks again every few seconds, so the bars follow a video whose colours change. The animated cover now gives the bars its colours too.
- **Fewer accidental touches.** A press that's held and then let go (the phone gripped, a palm or a thumb resting on the screen) no longer pauses, skips, likes, seeks or jumps to a lyric line; only a quick tap does. Buttons with their own action on a hold (Rotate) keep it. A lyric line also isn't jumped to when the touch slid sideways or another finger was on the screen.
- **The visualizer is back inside the side margins,** at its taller height (it reached the screen's edges since 0.46.2).
