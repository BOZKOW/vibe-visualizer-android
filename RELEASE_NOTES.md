Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.41.5 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.41.4 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Lines too big for their space

- **A line too tall for the room now scrolls with the singing**, in the lyrics' own timing, in the small window and on its side (and anywhere else it doesn't fit): from its start to its end, with the words being sung always in view. In the Focus style it wasn't followed at all and slid out of sight.
- **Its translation comes into view too**: by the time the last words are sung, the line has scrolled to its end, translation included.
- **Lyrics without word timing scroll too**, by the time of the line (a tall one stayed at its first row).
- **Two lines sung at once in the Focus style stay in view together** when they don't fit one under the other.

### The phone on its side

- **The lyrics have the right side to themselves**, top to bottom: where it plays, the notices and all the controls moved to the left, with translate and romanize beside the song's name.
- **Without a Canvas**, all of it sits over the big cover, on a soft shade. When the controls hide, the cover has its middle back.
- **With a Canvas**, the cover shrinks to make room for the controls and grows back when they hide. Where there's too little room for it, it fades out until they do.
- **Notices can take two lines** there, as the left side is narrower.
