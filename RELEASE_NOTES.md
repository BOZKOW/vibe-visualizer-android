Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.51.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.50.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Lines being sung stay bright.** In the Focus style a line coming in faded in slowly, so its first words were already being sung while it was still dim and blurred. It now arrives at full strength, background vocals included. In every style, the clear area around the lines briefly swept up the screen whenever a second line came in or one of two ended, dimming the lines being sung for a moment. It no longer does.
- **Smooth scrolling in the small window with line-synced lyrics.** A line taller than the window now moves down with its fill smoothly, and a new line glides in, instead of creeping in little jolts. Word-synced lyrics there scroll as before.
- **Your order of lyrics sources decides.** A source lower in your list (KuGou, for example) was used over Spicy Lyrics' community lyrics whenever it had word-by-word timing and they only had line timing, and a slow answer from the top of your list could lose to a quicker one below it. Now the first synced lyrics from the top of your list win. Settings → Lyrics → Word timing over your order brings back the old way. Songs are looked up once more so this applies to them too.
- **The visualizer follows the Canvas's colours quickly.** It takes them a quarter of a second after the video starts and looks again every 1.5 seconds (it was every 5), using what's on screen now instead of a mix of the last few seconds, and the bars change colour in 0.3 seconds instead of blending for nearly a second.
- **Notices stay until you've read them.** A long notice now stays on screen until it has slid to its end, instead of being swapped for the next one or going away midway. In Settings, long notices slide too (they didn't move there at all).
