Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.60.5 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.60.4 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Smoother cover transitions:** cover travel keeps a stable rendering surface, and changing the available lyrics height reuses the existing text layout. Animation preferences now apply consistently.
- **Live notice glass:** notices blur the actual cover, song text and player content behind them while keeping the dark glass tint.
- **Up next artwork:** queue thumbnails and Spotify image addresses are preserved, with a matched artwork lookup when the queue provides none. Changing the next song also clears its previous thumbnail.
