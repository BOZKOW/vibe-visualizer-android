Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.45.6 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.45.5 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Widget lyrics no longer drawn far too small at a larger display size on Samsung phones.** One UI Home reports the widget's size at the phone's default display size; the app corrected that in both directions, and at a larger display size (Settings → Display → Screen zoom) it made the widget wider than the screen, so the lyrics came out tiny. It's now only corrected at a smaller display size, as in Spicy Lyrics Mobile 0.19.0.
