Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.43.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.43.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### On its side

- **A shorter blend on the Canvas**: the video stays sharp over about 60% of its side and only then blurs into the background. The blend had started so early that it covered nearly half of the video.
- **Settings have room**: the close button and the search now share one row, so about three rows of the list fit instead of one and a half, and the page's title lines up with them.
- **Notifications are frosted** like the seek bar and the buttons, so nothing sharp shows through them.
- **The Focus style no longer jumps**: a line being sung used to slide up a moment before its end, to make room for the next line, and then move again. It now holds still until it's sung, as it does upright. Two singers at once still make room for each other.

### Focus style

- **No more flash when lyrics change mid-song**: when a translation arrived, all the lines showed for a moment at half strength and flew off. This happened upright too.
