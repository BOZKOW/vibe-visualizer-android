Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.57.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.56.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Everything from your phone.** The app no longer uses Spotify's Web API or a Spotify login. The Spotify app is followed through Android, as any music app: the song, where it is, its cover and the controls come straight from it. Shuffle, repeat and the heart are the Spotify app's own buttons, with its icons. The Canvas and the lyrics sources that need Spotify's song ID read it from what the Spotify app tells Android. Your server is only asked for what needs it (Spicy Lyrics, Spotify's own lyrics, your Canvas). Notification access is needed, as before. Music playing on another device shows while the Spotify app on this phone shows it.
- **Spotify's DJ** is recognized on this path too: its note and its own picture.
- **Glass as in Spicy Lyrics Mobile 0.19:** darker and calmer, a thin ring and light edges over the blur, its colour being what shows through (the Canvas's or the cover's).
- **Notices float over the song** at the top of the Lyrics screen instead of pushing it down.
- **The controls sit higher** on the Lyrics screen, further from the bottom edge.
- **From Spicy Lyrics Mobile 0.20:**
  - Chinese is romanized by word, so a character takes the reading its word uses (行走 → xíng zǒu, 长大 → zhǎng dà).
  - Keep lyrics longer (Settings → Lyrics): found lyrics kept for 30 days instead of 7.
  - Auto-scroll for lyrics without timing: Basic moves with the song and holds still through quiet parts; Off leaves them where you put them.
  - Romanization under the line is the default for new installs.
  - The moving background follows the song's energy instead of its tempo and beats.
