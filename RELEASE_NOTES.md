Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.41.11 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.41.10 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### The small window

- **The lyrics scroll as they did in 0.41.5 again**: each move starts as the next line or row starts and takes about half a second, and a line too long for the window goes down with the singing. The scroll changes from 0.41.6 to 0.41.9 no longer apply to the small window. After a break, lines still slide into place instead of jumping.
