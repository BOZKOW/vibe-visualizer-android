Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.46.3 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.46.2 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Translations look like they do in Spicy Lyrics** (with Spicy Lyric Translator, the extension that adds them there): extra bold, a little smaller and tighter under each line, about half bright while their line isn't being sung, and filling brighter from left to right while it is. They still follow their line's side (the other singer's on the right) and its blur, and slide in as before.
