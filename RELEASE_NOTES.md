Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.46.4 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.46.3 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Focus style: the next line no longer comes in washed out.** When it starts while the line in focus is still going, it appeared under it dimmed and blurred, then brightened once it moved into place. It now looks the same all the way, only its words filling as they're sung.
- **Ambient mode: the way into it is smooth again.** The screen dropped to its slow, battery-saving refresh rate before the fade to black had finished, so the fade stuttered. It now waits until the fade is done; leaving Ambient still brings the full rate back at once.
