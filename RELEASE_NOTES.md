Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.46.2 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.46.1 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Edge to edge

- **Long song names and artists scroll out to the screen's edges** on the upright screens, fading out there, instead of stopping at the side margins. At rest they start where they always did. On the right they stop before the translate, romanize and like buttons when those are beside them.
- **The line at the top** ("THIS PHONE", where it plays) scrolls out to the screen's sides too when it's too long, its icon going along with the words, and never towards the camera.
- **Long notices stay on one line and scroll across their pill** instead of wrapping onto a second line (with Scroll long names off, or in Low performance mode, they still wrap). Their fixed room is a little taller for a notice with a second line of detail.
- **The bars run edge to edge** on the upright screens, and they're taller on the big cover screen.

### Fixed

- **Songs without a date no longer show the year 1904.** That's the zero date an M4A file without one carries; it now counts as no year.
