Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.41.10 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.41.9 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Turning the phone

- **The Canvas no longer stretches when you turn the phone**: going from upright to its side, or back, the video kept the proportions of the old size until the next song. It now fits again as soon as the screen turns.
