Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.39.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.39.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Breaks

- **The dots only show for a pause of under 10 seconds**, as in Spicy Lyrics. A pause of 10 seconds or more has none; with the Focus style the cover takes its place (Settings → Lyrics → Cover in long breaks), and stays until the next line comes in (it went 2 seconds early, leaving an empty screen). With the cover off, the line before the pause stays until the next one.
