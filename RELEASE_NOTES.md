Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.49.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.48.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Settings: the header turns into an island as you scroll.** At the top, Settings looks as it always did. Scroll down and the title steps away, the search box floats on its own as a solid island with the close button in it, and the list runs on behind it all the way to the top of the screen. On each page the title row does the same: the back arrow and the page's name on a small island that's just as long as the name.
- **Spotify comes back when Android closes it.** One UI sometimes closes the Spotify app in the background in the middle of a song. When that happens the app starts Spotify's player again, the way a headphone button would, without bringing Spotify to the front. If that doesn't work, a notice offers to open Spotify and says how to keep One UI from closing it. Settings → Music apps & Spotify → Bring Spotify back if Android closes it (needs Exact timing and controls from the Spotify app).
