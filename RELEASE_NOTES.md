Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.46.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.46.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Notices at the top now behave the same everywhere.** While one shows, the lyrics and the big cover both move down by the same fixed amount, however long the notice is (one line or two), and back up when it goes; the notice sits in that room. Before, it floated over the lyrics but pushed the cover down by its own height.
- **The lyrics re-place themselves as soon as their room changes size** (a notice coming or going, the controls hiding), smoothly, instead of keeping their old place until the next line: with a notice they sometimes moved and sometimes didn't.
