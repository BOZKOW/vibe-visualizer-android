Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.49.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.49.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **The lyrics no longer jump down and up at a line change.** Just after a new line started, the Spotify app's timing could put the song a fraction of a second back; the lyrics went back to the line before for it and then on again, so they jumped down and up, in every style including Focus. Now a step back that small is ignored for which line is sung (a real seek back still goes back), and the words' fill just waits for that moment instead of running backwards.
