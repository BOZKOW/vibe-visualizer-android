Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.53.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.52.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

A new look for the Lyrics screen, after Spicy Lyrics Mobile and its PC version, keeping what's yours:

- **The song at the top.** Upright, the Lyrics screen now starts with the song right under the status bar: a big cover, the name, the artist, and the heart for Spotify. The lyrics come under it, then the bars, the seek bar and the credits, and the controls when you wake them. Notices still come first. The top line ("THIS PHONE · SONG 1 OF 2") is left out on this screen; it stays on the others and on its side. Settings → Player screen → Cover at the top turns the cover off and leaves the name alone up there.
- **Translate and romanize in the round-button row.** On the Lyrics screen upright they come first among the buttons under the controls when the lyrics need them; your last picked buttons give way so the row keeps six.
- **The cover grows in long breaks.** With the Focus style and Cover in long breaks, the cover itself grows from the top into the lyrics' place, and flows back as the next line comes. The name stays where it is.
- **Up next under the artist.** On the Lyrics screen upright, the next song now shows as a small line under the artist (its room is always kept, so nothing moves). Elsewhere it's still the quiet notice at the top.
- **Background vocals are translated and romanized.** Each background vocal gets its own translation line under it, and with Romanization below its own romanization too; they tuck away and slide out with it. A background vocal in another script is now romanized even when its main line is in Latin letters.
- **Lyrics glide.** Let go of the lyrics while you scroll through them and they carry on and slow down, as any list does, instead of stopping dead. A touch stops them.
- **The DJ.** Spotify's DJ is now recognized by what the Spotify app names it ("DJ X", a few seconds long), so its note shows with the DJ's picture instead of a lookup for a song called "Up next"; the song it hands over to waits until it really plays.
- Landscape keeps its cover card.
