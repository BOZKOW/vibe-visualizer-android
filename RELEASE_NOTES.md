Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.58.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.57.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Controls like Spicy Lyrics Mobile:** its seek bar and its skip and play glyphs. Your round buttons stay.
- **Real covers again:** the Spotify app's grey placeholder disc no longer stands in for the cover.
- **Apple Music's animated covers** play in the cover at the top of the Lyrics screen.
- **Only errors are shown** at the top now; no warnings or other notices.
- **What's new after an update** shows in a card right away.
- **A stronger blur** behind the song at the top and the controls when a Canvas plays.
- **Every window blurs what's really behind it**, and Settings' floating titles no longer show the list sharp through them.
- **The DJ** no longer flickers between itself and the next song.
- **Release notes** are shorter, and points under points are indented in Help & about.
