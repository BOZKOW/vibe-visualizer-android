Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.60.2 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.60.1 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Playback recovery:** reconnects media-session callbacks and refreshes stale song information after returning to the app, with a local recovery check while it stays open.
- **Missing covers:** picks up Spotify artwork that arrives late and keeps a failed lookup from clearing a recovered cover.
