Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.51.2 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.51.1 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **A limit on skipping songs too fast.** Previous and next now take a press at most every third of a second, and at most five in four seconds; presses beyond that are let go. Each one asked Spotify again and started looking up a new song's lyrics, Canvas and cover, which could run into Spotify's limits and warm the phone. While songs fly by, a song's cover is now loaded only for the one you stop on, like its lyrics and Canvas already were.
- **On its side, the other singer's lines sit further right.** In duets, the lines of the singer shown on the right now end close to the screen's edge instead of well in from it; the bars and credits keep their margin.
