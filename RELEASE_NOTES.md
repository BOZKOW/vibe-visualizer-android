Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.60.6 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.60.5 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Temperature warnings removed:** no heat notice, full-screen interruption or PiP warning.
- **Duet waiting dots:** waiting dots appear on the same side as the upcoming singer, including right-side duet lines.
- **Settings layout fixes:** action labels stay on one line, crowded button groups wrap onto another row, and lyrics-source controls leave room for their text. Landscape search and category titles stay on one line too.
