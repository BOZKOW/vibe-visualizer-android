Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.45.5 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.45.4 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Animated profile pictures now play**: when a lyrics maker or uploader has a GIF as their picture, it moves in the credits instead of showing only its first frame. Animated WebP pictures work too, and so do Discord pictures that can move but whose address asks for a still copy.
- It freezes on its first frame in Low performance mode, in Ambient mode and while Settings is open. While a song plays it costs almost nothing, as Android draws the frames off the main thread and the lyrics screen is redrawing anyway.
- A picture that isn't square now fills its circle.
