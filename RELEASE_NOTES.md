Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.51.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.51.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Much less heat.** The seek bar smoothed its movement with an animation that never came to an end while a song played, and it ran at the screen's own rate: on a 120 Hz phone the whole app was drawn 120 times a second all the time, whatever Settings → Performance & battery → Limit frame rate said. The seek bar and long sliding names now move on the app's own frames, so the frame limit finally applies (60 a second by default; choose 120 or Off there if you'd rather have the smoothest motion over battery). On the test phone the app drew about 45 frames a second instead of 80, at the same smoothness.
- **No more heat from the Canvas's colours.** Since 0.51.0 the visualizer looked at the Canvas every 1.5 seconds, and each look changed the whole app's accent colour, which made every screen redraw for nearly a second. The app's colour now only follows a clear change in the video, at most every 6 seconds; the bars still follow it quickly. A frame that looks much like the last changes nothing, and the colours are worked out in the background.
- **Settings use less power while you scroll.** What's behind the floating search and titles is only captured while they float.
