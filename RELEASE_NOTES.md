Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.45.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.45.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Frost, upright

- **The seek bar, the buttons, the notifications and the Glass bars now take their colour from what's really behind them**: the background, the Canvas and the lyrics passing under them. They used to show a crop of the blurred cover, with a dark tint, which could look nothing like what was there. It costs nothing measurable, as it reuses pictures that are already blurred.

### On its side

- **The song name and the seek bar are back to their size from before 0.45**, with the Canvas as Spicy Lyrics lays it out.
