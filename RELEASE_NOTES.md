Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.58.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.58.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Your own seek bar is back**, as it was before 0.58.0.
- **Play and pause swap with your old quarter turn** instead of Spicy Lyrics Mobile's.
- **The glass takes a light touch of the song's colour** again, or the Canvas's while one plays.
- **Less drawing for nothing:** the lyrics and the seek bar rest while nothing changes, so a paused screen with nothing moving draws nothing.
