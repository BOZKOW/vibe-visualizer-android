Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.48.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.47.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

- **Duets: each singer's line goes its own way.** When one line is still being sung while the other singer's lines go on (a long held note, a backing line), it stays at the top until it ends, and the other lines scroll on under it instead of pushing it away or being pushed out of view. In the small window, where there's little room, only the row of it being sung stays at the top. A line that's only just ending no longer makes the lyrics jump back and forth in fast songs.
- **Settings in Android 17's style (Material 3 Expressive), edge to edge.** Each setting is its own rounded tile, with small gaps between them and rounder corners at the ends of each group, in both the Glass and the Android look. The lists now run from the top of the screen to the bottom: they slide up under the title and the search, fading out there instead of stopping in a hard line, and go on behind the navigation bar.
- **Better notices.** Each one starts with an icon for its kind (red: something's broken, yellow: waiting, white: good news). Over Settings they now cover the title row cleanly instead of sitting on top of the word "Settings".
- **The cover is in sight with the phone upright.** On the Lyrics screen a small cover sits beside the song's name (it steps aside while the big cover fills a long break). Settings → Player screen → Small cover beside the song name.
- **Landscape, edge to edge.** The cover runs the full height of the screen, and the lyrics, the bars and the credits reach the right-hand side, fading out there. The song, its buttons and the top line stay clear of the system bars.
- **Visualizer: steadier auto sensitivity.** It follows the music's louder parts as a whole instead of its single loudest moment: one loud hit no longer shrinks the bars for seconds, and quiet intros, fade-outs and the gaps between songs are no longer boosted until the bars jump on near-silence (4× at most now, not 8×).
- **Long names and notices slide more calmly.** Ones that are just a little too long slide as before; long ones now go at an easier pace.
