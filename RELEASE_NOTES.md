Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.46.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.45.6 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Animated covers (from Spicy Lyrics Mobile 0.19.0)

- **Tall animated covers.** On the big cover held upright (a song without lyrics), a record that has Apple Music's tall animated cover plays that one, and the cover grows from square to its taller shape as it starts. Elsewhere, and in Low performance mode, the cover stays square.
- **New setting: Animated cover before Canvas** (Settings → Player screen, under Animated covers; off by default). A song with both plays its animated cover on the big cover instead of its Canvas, and the Canvas only plays for songs without one. The Canvas waits a moment while the cover is looked up.

### Spicy Lyrics for more songs

- **Songs from other music apps and your own files can get Spicy Lyrics** without going through your Spotify account. Your server asks Spicy Lyrics by the song's ISRC when it's known, or finds the song by its title, artist, album and length, and only takes a match Spicy Lyrics calls exact, within 5 seconds of the song's length (Spicy Lyrics' API added this on 5 November). This needs the updated server; until it's updated, the app asks the old way, through your Spotify connection.

### Buttons

- **Six buttons under the controls**: a fifth one you pick, then Settings, still last.
- **Look up the lyrics again**, the new fifth button: clears this song's saved lyrics and searches your sources again.
- **Rotate the screen**, a new choice for any of the five: a tap turns the app on its side (or back upright) and it stays so, whatever the phone's rotation; hold it to follow the phone again.
- **Back to Spotify in one tap.** While your own files play and Spotify is connected, the line at the top says "THIS PHONE · ⇄ SPOTIFY": tap it to go back to Spotify.
